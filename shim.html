The Paragraph Drafting Coach

Brainstorm and plan an introduction with 3 helpful questions.

Learning Goal: Help outline your essay structure without writing it for you.
Act as a friendly middle school writing coach. I will give you my main idea or thesis for an essay about [Insert Topic], and you will ask me three guiding questions to help me write a strong introduction paragraph. Do not write the paragraph for me!

The Vocabulary Storyteller

Practice using new vocab words in a funny, short story.

Learning Goal: Create a memorable story context for weekly vocabulary words.
I am studying these middle school vocabulary words: [Insert Words]. Write a short, funny story (under 150 words) using all of these words. Bold each vocabulary word so I can see how they are used.

Book Character Interview

Bring your favorite literary characters to life.

Learning Goal: Engage in a deep Socratic discussion as a literary character.
Act as [Insert Character] from the book [Insert Book Name]. I want to interview you for a class project. I will ask you questions one by one. Start by introducing yourself and welcoming me.

The Step-by-Step Tutor

Get clues and step-by-step guidance on hard problems.

Learning Goal: Understand math operations instead of just getting the answer.
Act as an encouraging middle school math teacher. Do not give me the final answer directly. Help me solve this math problem step-by-step: [Insert Problem]. First, ask me what my first step should be.

Real-World Math Explainer

Find out how boring concepts are used in fun jobs or hobbies.

Learning Goal: Relate abstract math concepts to sports, cooking, or gaming.
How does the math concept [Insert Concept, e.g., ratios, fractions, slope] apply to a real-world activity like sports, video games, or baking? Give me two super cool, simple examples.

Word Problem Decoder

Translate confusing paragraph problems into clean math equations.

Learning Goal: Identify variables and operations in complex math stories.
